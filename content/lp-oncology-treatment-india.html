Frequently Asked Questions

Is the consultation free?

Globalmediicare offers free case-management assistance. Hospital consultations, investigations and treatment are subject to the healthcare provider's charges.

Can I send medical reports?

Yes. You can share relevant reports through WhatsApp or with the care team so the appropriate hospital can review the case.

Will you guarantee a treatment outcome?

No. Diagnosis, treatment decisions and outcomes depend on the treating doctors and the patient's individual condition.
